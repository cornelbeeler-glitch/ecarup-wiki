![smart-me Pico charging station](/img/smart-me-pico/01.png)

Pico connects directly to the cloud via Wi-Fi or mobile network. The integrated smart meter exports precise measurement data, which is signed during transactions and can be validated at any time.

In addition, Pico can be used for both static and dynamic load management, including phase balancing.

The charging station has the MID certification required for billing purposes.



[More information about Pico](https://wiki.smart-me.com/produkte/pico-ladestation)

[Commissioning and configuration guide](https://wiki.smart-me.com/konfiguration/inbetriebnahme/pico-konfiguration)

## eCarUp connection

### Prerequisite:

The Pico wallbox has been successfully installed by an electrician, connected to the internet and commissioned with smart-me. 

1.  Log in to the smart-me web portal. ([www.smart-me.com](http://www.smart-me.com))


![smart-me Pico – Figure 2](/img/smart-me-pico/02.png)

2\. Select the Pico wallbox you want in your smart-me portal and click the gear icon at the top right. 

![smart-me Pico – Figure 3](/img/smart-me-pico/03.png)

3\. Under Authentication, click eCarUP Backend so that the Pico is connected to the eCarUp account of the same name

![smart-me Pico – Figure 4](/img/smart-me-pico/04.png)

4\. Save the settings.

![smart-me Pico – Figure 5](/img/smart-me-pico/05.png)
