To use automatic payout, you need an eCarUp Premium licence.

### Creating an automatic payout plan in eCarUp:

With the automatic payout plan from eCarUp, the revenue from charging sessions is transferred regularly and without manual effort to the bank account the station operator has stored.

The following options are available:

- Quarterly

- Semi-annually

- Annually


### Activate the payout plan:

1.  Go to your eCarUp account and, under Account (Account), click on Overview (Übersicht).

2.  Select Set payout plan (Auszahlungplan setzen). 


![Automatic payout – Figure 1](/img/funktionen-automatische-auszahlung/01.png)

3\. Select the desired interval period. 

4\. Set the date for the next payout. From this date onwards, payouts follow the interval you selected in step 3. 

5\. Save (Speichern). 

![Automatic payout – Figure 2](/img/funktionen-automatische-auszahlung/02.png)

Info: We do not offer monthly automatic payouts because of the high transaction costs they would incur.
