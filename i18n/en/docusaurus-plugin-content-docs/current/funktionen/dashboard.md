## Yellow bar

If you see this yellow bar on your dashboard, you have not yet fully verified your account. As a result, your account may be restricted and payouts, deposits and charging sessions may be blocked.

Verify your account using the following guide:

![Dashboard – Figure 1](/img/funktionen-dashboard/01.png)

[Verify account](/stationsbetreiber/account-verifizieren)

### Charging station status

See the status of all stations connected to your account at a glance.
There are 4 status indicators in total: 

- Available (Verfügbar)
    Your station is connected and communicating with our backend.

- Connected (Verbunden)
    A car is plugged in and waiting for a charge to be started.

- Occupied (Besetzt)
    A car is connected to the station and a charge has been started.

- Out of service (Ausser Betrieb)
    The charging station is not communicating with us. 


![Dashboard – Figure 2](/img/funktionen-dashboard/02.png)

⚠️Important: With the status "Out of service" (ausser Betrieb), the station has no active communication with the backend. We automatically mark the station as "Out of service" (ausser Betrieb) after 15 minutes. If you would like to be notified of an interruption, take a look at the following setting: "[Alarm (Alarm)](/funktionen/wartung/alarme)".  

## Recent charging sessions

Under this item, you can see the most recent charging sessions that have taken place.
In this overview, you can see the following:

- Name of the charging station at which the charge took place.

- Revenue generated by this charge.

- Date and time of the charging session.

- How many hours the charge lasted and how many kWh the charge used. 


## Revenue

Here you can see how much money in your eCarUp account is ready for payout. As soon as you make a payout, the amount decreases. This is therefore only the current amount in your account.

If you see an amount under "Available soon" (Bald verfügbar), the money received is being checked by Stripe for money laundering. The check takes place within a few hours, after which the money should automatically be transferred to the regular revenue.
