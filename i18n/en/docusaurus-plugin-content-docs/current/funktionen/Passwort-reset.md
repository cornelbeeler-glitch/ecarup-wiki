This page describes how to reset your password on eCarUp.

<Embed src="https://demo.arcade.software/O26pAuFgHpDQSRr1ejif?embed&embed_mobile=tab&embed_desktop=inline&show_copy_link=true" aspect="1.730" title="Reset password" />

If you have an eCarUp account but can no longer log in, the most likely reason is that you entered the wrong password. eCarUp passwords do not expire. Once a password is set, only the account user can change it. eCarUp cannot see passwords and cannot change them.

If you have forgotten your password, click "Forgot password?" ("Passwort vergessen?").

After entering your email address, click "Send" ("Senden").

If you entered the email address correctly, an email from Smart-me, our parent company, should appear in your inbox.

Note: This email comes from Smart-me because your eCarUp account is linked to an account with our parent company, Smart-me.

If the email does not arrive, check two things:

- Check that you entered the correct email address above. No error message appears if the address is wrong. This is deliberate: it stops anyone from finding out whether an account exists and so helps prevent attacks.

- Also check your spam folder.

Click the link to go to a new page. Make sure the whole link is blue. If it is not, your email provider has not read the link correctly. In that case, copy the link and paste it into your browser.

Once you have clicked the link or pasted it into your browser, a new smart-me page should open. There you can set a new password.

If "Add a new password" ("Ein neues Passwort hinzufügen") does not appear, there are two possible reasons:

- You must not be logged in with another smart-me or ecarup account. Otherwise you will change the password of the account that is logged in.

- If the "Add a new password" ("Ein neues Passwort hinzufügen") screen does not appear, the link or URL may have been cut off. This happens mainly on iOS/Apple devices. You can spot the problem because the URL is not blue along its whole length. In this case, clicking the link will not let you reset your password. Copy the link and paste it into your browser instead.

To change your password, enter your new password here. Do not use the characters £ or $, because they can cause errors.

Then click "Save new password" ("Neues Passwort speichern") to set the new password.

You will be taken to the Smart-me login page. Do not log in there. Go back to the eCarUp login page instead.

Once you are back on the eCarUp login page, log in with your email address and your new password.
