To use price plans, you need an eCarUp Premium licence.

## Function

- A standard price plan is created so that a price can still be used as a reference if a price plan is invalid.

- Price plans can be individually adjusted to a time horizon.

- A penalty fee can be charged. Depending on the configuration, it becomes due during the charging session and is not a blocking fee.

- If a driver is accidentally included in two different price plans, the cheaper one is selected. 

- If an active price plan is changed (e.g. the kWh price is adjusted), its validity is moved to the next day. 

- The grace period starts when the charging session starts, regardless of whether a penalty tariff is active or not. 

- Does not apply to [Roaming](/funktionen/roaming)


## Important for station operators

- Dynamic tariffing on a kWh basis is not yet supported. 


- The new pricing policy, including the penalty tariff, should be communicated above all to users who only use RFID cards. 


## Important for drivers

The app must have at least the following version:

- Android version: 2.5.8 

- iOS version: 2.7.1
