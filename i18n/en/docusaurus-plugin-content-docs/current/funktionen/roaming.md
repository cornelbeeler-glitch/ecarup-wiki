eCarUp is a member of Hubject, the largest charging roaming network worldwide. Charging roaming allows drivers to use the charging stations of other charging networks. The eCarUp platform provides CPO functionality, and station owners can optionally make their charging station available in other networks.

![Roaming – Figure 1](/img/funktionen-roaming/01.png)

ATTENTION: The prices for your station will vary, in some cases considerably, across the respective partner networks. You only select a price plan, which is transmitted to the partner network. The final pricing for the end customer is the responsibility of the respective partner network. Neither eCarUp nor you as the station owner have any influence on this.

As a result, your charging station will cost different amounts on different charging networks.

! However, it is still possible to use our [instant payment function](/fahrer/sofort-bezahl-funktion) to activate charging sessions locally and thus possibly avoid more expensive roaming costs. We therefore ask operators of charging stations to label their charge points properly using a [QR code](/stationsbetreiber/qr-code). 

### How do I activate Hubject

Hubject can be found in the eCarUp account under 

- Stations (Stationen) - 

- Management (Verwaltung) - via the three dots on the side - 

- Edit (Bearbeiten) - then scroll down - 

- Hubject (roaming network) (Hubject (Roaming-Netzwerk)) - Here Hubject can be activated and the price can be defined. 


Important: the location (address) must be set. 

![Roaming – Figure 2](/img/funktionen-roaming/02.png)

## Station owners

### CPO functionality

As a station owner, you can also make your station available to the users of partner networks.  The requirement is that the station has the status "Public" ("Öffentlich") and is therefore accessible 24/7. 

You can activate the integration of your charging stations in the [eCarUp web portal](https://admin.ecarup.com/authentication/login). It will then take approx. 2-3 days until it is displayed on the partner networks.

Note: Via Hubject, it is only possible to pass on a price per kWh. It is not possible to charge a parking fee.

### Where can I find the EVSE ID:

Where the price for roaming is activated, our system creates the EVSE ID after saving.

## Price plans

Price plans for roaming look as follows:

LowCHF (0.25 /  kWh)
MediumCHF (0.50 /  kWh)
HighCHF (0.70 /  kWh)
ExtraCHF (0.95 /  kWh)

LowEUR (0.25 /  kWh)
MediumEUR (0.50 /  kWh)
HighEUR (0.70 /  kWh)
ExtraEUR (0.95 /  kWh)

10% credit card fees are deducted from each of these.

![Roaming – Figure 5](/img/funktionen-roaming/05.png)

We cannot say what flat rates or fees the respective platform provider adds for a charge.
These prices are for the station manager, so 10% of the specified price plan is always deducted for them.
