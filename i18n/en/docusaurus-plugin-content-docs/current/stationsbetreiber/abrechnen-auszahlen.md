With eCarUp billing by credit and debit card, charging stations can be billed fully automatically and with minimal effort. This is possible for 1:1 billing in rental arrangements or condominium ownership associations (STWEG), but also in
semi-public (e.g. visitor parking spaces) or fully public use cases.

## Billing by credit card

The eCarUp app has a fully automated payment function. The amounts are charged to a credit card. 

### Credit card transactions

If a chargeable transaction takes place at an eCarUp station, the corresponding amount (based on the price set by the station owner) is charged to the credit card stored by the driver directly after the charging session. The credit is transferred fully automatically to an account of the station owner. For this service, eCarUp charges a 10% fee, which covers all credit card and transaction fees up to the payout to a bank account of the station owner's choice. 

(Payouts can only be made from an amount of 10.- CHF/EURO)

Transactions below CHF 0.50 are not processed by the system.

The following credit cards are accepted: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club and JCB

## Payouts

Revenue generated is credited to the station owner. In the web portal, you can store your preferred IBAN and request the payout of the credit at any time. As a station owner, you must provide some personal details in accordance with regulatory requirements, due to the [Know Your Customer principle](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Know_your_customer) that applies in Europe.

When the credit is paid out, station owners receive a confirmation of the transferred amount by email.  Occasionally, it may end up in the spam folder.

### Failed payout

The payout can be tracked at any time in the web portal under Account (Account), and the receipt can be resent to the email address of the account owner. (eCarUp Portal (Web) --> Account (Account) \--> Balance history (Saldoverlauf) \--> Resend (Erneut Senden) (link on the right-hand side) 

If the status shows Failed (Fehlgeschlagen), please contact our support. (Email to [support@ecarup.com](mailto:support@ecarup.com))

If, in exceptional cases, the payout is not processed automatically, in most cases this is because the bank cannot process the credit transfer.  In such a case, please contact your bank.
