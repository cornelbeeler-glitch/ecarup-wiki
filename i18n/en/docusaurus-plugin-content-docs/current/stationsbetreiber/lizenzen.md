## General information

The eCarUp licenses can be obtained in two ways.

- Purchase them by credit card directly in the web portal.

- By invoice, if you want to buy 10-year licenses. If you have any questions, contact your installation partner or write to [verkauf@ecarup.com](mailto:verkauf@ecarup.com). 

You can find more information on pricing here on our website. [Homepage](https://web.ecarup.com/preise-ladestationen-backend/).

- If you have not already done so, please add a payment method and your address.

- Select the desired term (Monthly (Monatlich) or Yearly (Jährlich)).

- Enter the number of connectors. Note that all connectors must be licensed. A mixed form (Free and Premium) is not possible.

- Accept the terms of use.

- Click Buy now with obligation to pay (Jetzt kostenpflichtig kaufen).

- You automatically receive a receipt at the email address of your account (this cannot be changed).

⚠️Note: Please log out and log back in so that the new features are available.

![Licenses – Figure 2](/img/stationsbetreiber-lizenzen/02.png)

## Adding licenses to an existing subscription

- Log in to the web portal and navigate to Account --> Licenses (Account --> Lizenzen).

- For the desired subscription, select the three dots on the right and click Add licenses (Lizenzen hinzufügen).

- Enter the additional quantity.

- Accept the terms of use.

- Select Add licenses (Lizenzen hinzufügen).

- The purchased license will appear on the receipt with the next billing. 

- ⚠️Note: Please log out and log back in so that the new license is available. 

![Licenses – Figure 3](/img/stationsbetreiber-lizenzen/03.png)

## Cancellation

- Log in to the web portal and navigate to Account --> Licenses (Account --> Lizenzen).

- For the desired subscription, select the three dots on the right and click Cancel (Kündigen).

- The licenses are not automatically renewed after the end date and remain active until then. Afterwards, please log out and log back in to check whether the status has changed to "Cancelled" ("Gekündigt").
