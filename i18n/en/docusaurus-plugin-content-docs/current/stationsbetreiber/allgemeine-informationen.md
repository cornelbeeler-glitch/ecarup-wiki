## Info:

Think of eCarUp as a platform on which various station operators can connect their charge points with us in order to operate them independently and according to their own conditions. We – the eCarUp backend – are essentially the technology supplier and provide the software system for the billing and management of EV charging stations. We are not the station owner and we do not sell electricity.

As a consequence, the station owner is responsible for operating their charge points. This includes, for example, the following topics:

- Prices for energy and parking time (including VAT rate)

- Opening hours

- Parking management (e.g. fine management)

- Maintenance, servicing and care of the charge points

- Labelling (QR code)

- Information about the station, including contact details


If you have problems using a charging station with our backend, our ability to help is limited. In any case, it makes sense to contact the station operator directly. 

If you want to connect and operate charging stations with the eCarUp platform, you can find help in the following places:

- You can find a list of the stations we have tested here: [OCPP charging stations](/ocpp-ladestationen)

- You can find help with the commissioning of your OCPP station here: [Commissioning an OCPP station](/stationsbetreiber/inbetriebnahme-ocpp-station)

- The configuration of the [most important use cases in the wiki](/anwendungsfaelle)

- Verification of your account (important for receiving money): [Verify account](/stationsbetreiber/account-verifizieren)

- Our premium offering for additional features: [Features](/funktionen)

- And much more in our [FAQ](/faq)


eCarUp itself does not do project business (consulting and installation of charge points); instead, we train a growing network of partners. You can find all partners trained in our technology [on the website](https://web.ecarup.com/partner-de/). Alternatively, you are welcome to make your trusted installation company aware of our solution – we are happy to train new partners. 

## Account structure:

At eCarUp, we recommend creating one account per property. This has the following advantages:

- All revenue flows transparently to the respective properties (otherwise all revenue flows into one account and these amounts have to be split up using a report (additional administrative and accounting effort).

- Should a client actually no longer wish to obtain the service via the billing service provider, the existing account can be handed over by means of the e-mail address (and a password change) without any further configuration.

- Using roles and permissions, the property accounts can be linked to a master account. You can find the guide for this here: [Roles and permissions](/funktionen/rollen-rechte)

- As soon as a smart-me ZEV exists, the account and the OCPP station must be separated.

## eCarUp in combination with smart-me (ZEV)

Note: When a smart-me account is created, an eCarUp account with the same name is automatically created, and vice versa. 

The portals are licensed differently, and if handled incorrectly, licences could become necessary, particularly on the smart-me side. To avoid this, follow the rules below.

OCPP stations:

- With OCPP stations, please use an additional, separate eCarUp account (see guide below) alongside the existing smart-me account to avoid additional Professional licences at smart-me. (1x Professional licence per OCPP charging station connector)

- If there is no smart-me installation in your building, you can create a new account of your choice.


smart-me Pico charging stations:

- You can integrate the Pico charging station into an existing smart-me account. Log in to eCarUp with your existing smart-me account details.

- If there is no smart-me installation in your building, you can create a new account of your choice.
