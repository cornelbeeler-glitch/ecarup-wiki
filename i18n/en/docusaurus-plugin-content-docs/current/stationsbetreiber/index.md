## Managing the Premium licence in the web portal

You can activate the Premium licence in the eCarUp web portal. You will find all the important information on this at the following link: [Licences](/stationsbetreiber/lizenzen)

 Important information: As an eCarUp partner, you can order 10-year licences directly from us. To do so, simply contact [verkauf@ecarup.com](mailto:verkauf@ecarup.com).
