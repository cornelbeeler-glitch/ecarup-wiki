### When and why does my account need to be verified?

### Extension of the Know Your Customer principle (Fifth EU Anti-Money Laundering Directive)

The Know Your Customer principle (KYC) requires companies to verify the identity of their customers in order to prevent illegal activities such as money laundering and to comply with regulatory requirements. 

As part of the fight against terrorist financing, the EU tightened the transparency of financial transactions in 2020 by implementing the Fifth Anti-Money Laundering Directive. As a result, for eCarUp station owners the identity of the account holder (private individual or legal entity) must be established at all times and verified by entering identification information. You can find more information about the directive on this page: [https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/de/STATEMENT\_18\_3429](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/de/STATEMENT_18_3429).

Due to stricter European and Swiss anti-money laundering laws, all connected Stripe accounts must undergo an extended identity, mandate and ownership check in 2026. Stripe automatically compares your entries in the background with the national commercial registers:

- CH [https://www.zefix.ch](https://www.zefix.ch/)

- DE: [https://www.handelsregister.de](https://www.handelsregister.de/)

- AT: [https://www.firmenbuchgrundbuch.at](https://www.firmenbuchgrundbuch.at) 


### Verifying your account for Switzerland and European countries (SEPA)

This step is only required if money is collected via our platform. If all charging is free of charge, verification is not necessary.

Legal information (Rechtliche Angaben)

- Log in to the [eCarUp web portal](https://admin.ecarup.com/authentication/login) with your charging station operator account

- In the menu, navigate to Account --> Overview (Übersicht), go to the Legal information (Rechtliche Angaben) section and click Edit (Bearbeiten) next to 1. Set up account type (Art des Konto einrichten).

- Important: Changing the account type from private individual to company afterwards is only possible via our support.

## The following documents can be used for a successful verification

Documents for identity verification

(one document is sufficient in each case)

Make sure that the identity, first name and last name match the submitted document exactly.

- Reisepass / Passport

- Führerschein / Driver license

- Identitätskarte / ID card (scans of front and back are required)

- Aufenthaltsbewilligung / Residence permit


Documents for address verification

(one document is sufficient in each case)

Make sure that the address matches the submitted document exactly.

- Wohnsitzbestätigung / Residence certificate

- Rechnung von Energieversorgern /  Utility bill

- Kontoauszug  / Statement from a financial institution

- Offizielles Schreiben einer Behörde / Government issued letter


Company documents

(one document is sufficient in each case)

- MwSt-Registrierung / VAT registration

- Gründungsurkunde  / Articles of incorporation

- Jahresabschluss / Financial statement

- Handelsregistereintrag / Commercial register entry

- Handelsregisterauszug / Commercial register extract

## Why do I have to verify my account again from summer 2026?

If an account is verified after 16.06.2026, this does not need to be taken into account.

Why does the account have to be verified again? 

Reason: Verification of the ownership of your company. 

Due to stricter European and Swiss anti-money laundering laws, all connected Stripe accounts must undergo an extended identity, mandate and ownership check in 2026. 

Stripe automatically compares your entries in the background with the national commercial registers

- CH [https://www.zefix.ch](https://www.zefix.ch/)

- DE: [https://www.handelsregister.de](https://www.handelsregister.de/)

- AT: [https://www.firmenbuchgrundbuch.at](https://www.firmenbuchgrundbuch.at) 


If there are discrepancies between the register and the persons or company data stored in the Stripe account, the system raises an alert. Stripe now strictly requires all beneficial owners entered in the register as well as the managing directors with signing authority to be recorded.

Step 1. 

In your eCarUp account, go to the legal information (Rechtliche Angaben) and click "Verify (Verifizieren)"; the Stripe onboarding page will open in a new window.  

![Verify account – Figure 22](/img/stationsbetreiber-account-verifizieren/22.png)

1.  ### What must additionally be specified


### 1.1. Companies (GmbH, AG)

Stripe automatically compares your entries in the background with the national commercial registers

- CH [https://www.zefix.ch](https://www.zefix.ch/)

- DE: [https://www.handelsregister.de](https://www.handelsregister.de/)

- AT: [https://www.firmenbuchgrundbuch.at](https://www.firmenbuchgrundbuch.at) 




- 1.  Company data: Exact company name, address and UID (company identification number), character for character as registered in the commercial register.

    2.  Enter either a beneficial owner or the management.

        - Beneficial owner: Every person who directly or indirectly holds 25 % or more of the company shares must be entered with name, date of birth and private residential address. 


Tip for public limited companies (AG): Since shareholders are not publicly visible in the commercial register, the beneficial owner information entered at Stripe is initially based on your self-declaration. However, for later random checks by Stripe, you must have a current extract of your internal share register ready. If Stripe requests it and the data does not match your entry, the account will be permanently blocked. 

- 2.  -   Management (Directors/Executives): The managing directors entered in the register must also be recorded with their private data. (To comply with AML laws, if your company has no owners with more than 25 % shareholding, Stripe must instead collect information about the board.)


### 1.2. Foundations / non-profit organisations (Non-Profit)

Non-profit organisations have no commercial owners (shareholders). The principle "control instead of ownership" therefore applies here.

1.  1.  In the Stripe onboarding, make sure to select the type "Non-profit organisation (Gemeinnützige Organisation)" for the company. 

    2.  When asked about beneficial owners, select the option "Continue with no owners (Weiter ohne Eigentümer)".

    3.  Enter the data of the organisation as well as the private data (name, date of birth, residential address, ID photo) of at least one executive with signing authority (e.g. association president, board member or managing director)


### 1.3. Government agencies / authorities (cantons, municipalities)

1.  Enter the responsible employee as the account representative (Representative). This person must verify themselves in the normal way with their ID/passport.

2.  Since a municipality has no owners, select "No owners (Keine Eigentümer vorhanden)" for the UBOs (beneficial owners).

3.  The most important step: After submission, the dashboard will display an error because of the placeholders ("Failed Verification"). This is normal.

4.  Now contact eCarUp support at [support@ ecarup.com](mailto:support@ecarup.com) with the subject "Account verification" and the following content: Hello, we are a government agency and we would need the ID of the Stripe account for the account: xxxxx

5.  With the Stripe ID, please fill in a document with the following information (step 6.) and send it back to us. 

6.  Requirements for the letter: The document must be printed on the official letterhead of the municipality/canton, contain the Stripe account ID and be signed by a higher-level authority (e.g. the mayor or municipal president) who is not the account administrator themselves. The submission must be made from an official authority e-mail address (e.g. name@gemeinde.ch). The Stripe compliance team then activates the account manually.
