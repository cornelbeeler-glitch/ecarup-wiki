## Guide

<div className="row">
<div className="col col--6">

eCarUp charging stations can also be unlocked easily by users without the eCarUp app.

1\. Scan the QR code on the charging station with a QR code scanner of your choice or your phone camera. You will be redirected to a website.

2\. Select Continue as guest (Als Gast fortfahren)

</div>
<div className="col col--6 text--center">

![Instant payment function – Figure 1](/img/fahrer-sofort-bezahl-funktion/01.jpg)

</div>
</div>

<div className="row">
<div className="col col--8">

3. You will then see information about the station and its costs.

4\. Activate the station using a credit card or Twint.

Note: Enter your credit or debit card details or use Apple & Google Pay.

5. Fill in the email address field (for sending the receipt and the permanent link).

6. Then click Activate (Aktivieren). The charging session starts.

Caution: Since we do not know the driver, we reserve CHF 60.- or 80.- on the credit card (calculated based on the amount set by the station operator) and refund the difference once the charging session has ended.

If charging is unsuccessful, the blocked amount is released automatically. eCarUp does not unlawfully retain any credit.

</div>
<div className="col col--4 text--center">

![Instant payment function – Figure 2](/img/fahrer-sofort-bezahl-funktion/02.jpg)

</div>
</div>

<div className="row">
<div className="col col--6">

7. You are taken to the overview and see the current charging session. (Note: this live data may vary depending on the station manufacturer.)

8\. Fill in the email address field again (for sending the receipt and the permanent link) and click Send (Senden).

9. End charging session (Ladevorgang beenden)

⚠️Important: Some phones store the browser content (cache). If you accidentally close the browser, you can scan the QR code again with your phone camera to return to this overview.

</div>
<div className="col col--6 text--center">

![Instant payment function – Figure 3](/img/fahrer-sofort-bezahl-funktion/03.jpg)

</div>
</div>

<div className="row">
<div className="col col--6">

10. Once you have ended the charging session, you can download the receipt. You can see what an eCarUp receipt looks like after the charging session at the following [LINK](/fahrer/abrechnen-mit-debit-kreditkarte).

</div>
<div className="col col--6 text--center">

![Instant payment function – Figure 4](/img/fahrer-sofort-bezahl-funktion/04.jpg)

</div>
</div>

## Email with the deactivation link

If you entered your email address in the form above, you will receive an email immediately after charging starts with a permanent link,  which you can use to return to the started charging session at any time.

![Instant payment function – Figure 5](/img/fahrer-sofort-bezahl-funktion/05.png)
