## Driver notifications

### Failed payment

If a payment with the credit or debit card stored in eCarUp fails, you will receive a notification. 

You can find more information at the following [link](/fahrer/fehlgeschlagene-zahlung).

### Cancelled payment

If a payment with the credit or debit card stored in eCarUp fails and settling the payment repeatedly fails, you will receive an email. Please contact us at support@ecarup.com. 

### Payment method expiring

The stored payment method (credit or debit card) expires in one month. To ensure that payments continue to be processed smoothly, the payment information should be updated in the app in good time. 

You can find more information at the following [link](/fahrer/ecarup-app). 

## Driver statistics

### Monthly overview

The monthly overview is sent automatically to the driver's email address at the beginning of the following month. 

⚠️ Important:

1.  At least 1 paid charging session must have taken place. 

2.  We cannot influence the tariffs or the information about the charging station operators.

3.  This version is currently only available in English.

4.  The overview is not an invoice.

## Web portal

Log in to our [web portal](https://admin.ecarup.com/authentication/login) with your eCarUp account.

1.  All payment transactions are listed, including their payment status

2.  Under the menu item "History" you can create your driver statistics.

3.  RFID cards can also be added via the web portal.


![General information – Figure 3](/img/fahrer-fahrerstatistik/03.png)

Drivers can create their "driver statistics" ("Fahrerstatistiken") as follows:

1.  Under the "Driver" ("Fahrer") tab, click on History ("Verlauf / History"). 

2.  Select the start and end date and click on the refresh arrow to update the list.

3.  Optionally, you can also create an export to an Excel file.


You can find more information on calibration law (Eichrecht) and on verifying charging sessions on the following [wiki page](/sonstige-informationen/eichrecht-in-deutschland).
