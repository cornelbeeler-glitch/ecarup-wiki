⚠️ Payment is fully automated. The amounts are booked directly through our payment service provider.

## Transactions by debit & credit card

When a chargeable transaction takes place at an eCarUp station, the corresponding amount (based on the price set by the station owner) is charged to the debit or credit card stored by the driver immediately after the charging session. The funds are transferred to an account of the station owner fully automatically.

Transactions under CHF 0.50 are not processed by the system.

The following debit & credit cards are accepted: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, Diners Club and JCB

### Payment

To pay for charging sessions with the eCarUp app, users store a debit or credit card in the app under Account. The amount for each charging session is debited from the stored card immediately after deactivation. 

The amount is calculated based on consumption (you only pay for what you have charged), using the price for energy and parking time defined by the station operator. 

No further fees apply.

The driver automatically receives a receipt by e-mail immediately after the charging session. Occasionally, it may end up in the spam folder.

⚠️ Important: The station owner stores the VAT information based on their own conditions (private individuals or foundations, for example, are not subject to VAT).

### Were additional charges listed on your credit card statement in addition to the charging price?

This may affect drivers in the EU area and must be clarified with the credit card provider. Most card providers' terms and conditions then contain, for example, the following sentence:

"Payment transactions in which amounts in euros are transferred to an account in an EEA contracting state that does not belong to the euro currency area and in which the currency conversion is carried out."

Please contact your card provider in this case.

Learn more about the security of financial transactions in the article [Stripe](/stationsbetreiber/account-verifizieren).
