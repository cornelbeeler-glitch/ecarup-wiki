## App

### Releasing failed payments in the app

If a payment with the credit or debit card stored in eCarUp has failed (e.g. because the card was blocked or had insufficient funds at the time in question), no further charges can be made with this eCarUp account until the outstanding amount has been settled.

The following reasons can lead to this:

- The stored card was blocked at the time of the charge attempt

- The stored card did not have sufficient funds at the time of the charge attempt

- The stored card has expired

- Other problem 

You have the following options:

- Update the credit card information in the app.

- A "Failed payment" ("Fehlgeschlagene Zahlung") button is displayed, which you can use to trigger the previously failed payment again once your credit card is working again.

Info: This is a security mechanism to protect the station operators.

## Changing / adding the credit card

If you want to change the credit card in the app, you can do so under "Account -> Payment methods" ("Account -> Zahlungsmittel").

Then tap "Edit" ("Bearbeiten") (if a credit card has already been added) and enter your new credit card.

If no credit card has been added, you can tap the "+" to add one.
