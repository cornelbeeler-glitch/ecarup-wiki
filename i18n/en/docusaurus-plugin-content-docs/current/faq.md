<details id="wie-werden-die-einnahmen-meiner-stationen-verrechnet">
<summary>How is the revenue from my station(s) settled?</summary>

Revenue you earn is automatically credited to your eCarUp account. Store your bank details in your account so you can trigger a payout to the linked bank account at any time. (Payouts are only possible from 10 CHF/€.)

We charge 10% on all gross financial transactions processed via the eCarUp platform. This includes all credit card fees. The commission is withheld directly and automatically during the payment process (via app or credit card).

</details>

<details id="kann-ich-einen-kommentar-auf-der-auszahlungsquittung-hinterlegen">
<summary>Can I add a comment to the payout receipt?</summary>

Yes, you can also add a comment or commission to the receipt. In addition, you can trigger the receipt again in the web portal. More information on this [page](/stationsbetreiber/abrechnen-auszahlen#comment-field-for-the-payout-receipt). 

</details>

<details id="kann-ich-mehrere-ladestationen-mit-meinem-account-verwalten">
<summary>Can I manage several charging stations with my account?</summary>

Yes, you can manage an unlimited number of charging stations in your account.

</details>

<details id="was-passiert-wenn-ein-fahrer-einen-schaden-an-meiner-ladestation-verursacht">
<summary>What happens if a driver causes damage to my charging station?</summary>

Liability insurance is required and regulated by road traffic law. It covers damage that drivers cause to others with their vehicle (damage to persons and property, as well as loss of earnings resulting from such damage).

eCarUp therefore excludes any liability for damage to charging stations caused by third parties.

</details>

<details id="meine-ladestation-hat-den-status-ausser-betrieb-was-heisst-das">
<summary>My charging station has the status "Out of order" (Ausser Betrieb). What does that mean?</summary>

The charging station cannot be used at the moment. This status is displayed if a charging station has not been able to connect to the backend in the last few minutes, e.g. because the Wi-Fi was interrupted or the station had no internet connection for some other reason. So first check the Wi-Fi / internet connection on site.

Another reason could be a power outage on site, which means the station cannot be used.

</details>

<details id="sind-die-einnahmen-durch-die-vermietung-von-e-ladestationen-steuerpflichtig">
<summary>Is the revenue from renting out EV charging stations taxable?</summary>

Yes, as a rule the revenue from renting is taxable for the provider. Depreciation deductions for the charging station and deductions for expenses (costs directly related to generating the income) are possible.

Please check the national and cantonal regulations with your responsible tax office.

</details>

<details id="muss-meine-ladestation-mit-ecarup-öffentlich-zugänglich-sein">
<summary>Does my charging station have to be publicly accessible with eCarUp?</summary>

No, as the station owner you can flexibly decide who may charge at your station, when and at what price. This gives you full control over the use of the station. The access type and the special users can be adjusted to your wishes at any time in the [web portal](/stationsbetreiber/webportal) at no additional cost.

</details>

<details id="was-sind-nutzergruppen--spezielle-nutzer">
<summary>What are user groups / special users?</summary>

Basically, an eCarUp station can be operated in three different modes.

Public (Öffentlich): All users (via the eCarUp app, instant payment, partner network if applicable) can use your charging station. These are the users you can earn the most money with.

Limited: Only special users configured by you can charge at your charging station, at the prices and times you set. For example, choose people from your circle of friends, your neighbourhood or your employees with whom you want to share your charging station at a particularly low price. No eCarUp commission is charged for people with whom you share free of charge.

Private (Privat): Only you as the station owner have access to the charging station.

You can also differentiate these modes flexibly over time. For example, you share your charging stations in the employee car park with your employees at a preferential price during office hours (e.g. CHF 0.25 per kWh and CHF 0.00 per hour). In the evening and at weekends, the public can also charge, but at a different price (e.g. CHF 0.35 per kWh and CHF 0.50 per hour).

</details>

<details id="darf-ich-energie-an-meiner-ladestation-weiterverkaufen">
<summary>Am I allowed to resell energy at my charging station?</summary>

In Switzerland: Yes! The Federal Council stated this in its response to a parliamentary interpellation ([16.3660](https://www.parlament.ch/de/ratsbetrieb/suche-curia-vista/geschaeft?AffairId=20163660)).

Outside Switzerland, follow the country-specific regulations.

</details>

<details id="wie-kann-ich-meinen-ecarup-account-löschen">
<summary>How can I delete my eCarUp account?</summary>

**Deleting your account in the eCarUp app**

- Log in to the eCarUp app with your username and password.

- Click Account -> Delete account (Account löschen) (This step cannot be undone.)  

⚠️Caution! Do not do this if a smart-me account with the same name already exists. 

**Deleting your account in the smart-me web portal**

- Log in to [www.smart-me.com](https://web.smart-me.com/login/)  with your eCarUp username and password.

- Make sure that the account contains no meters other than the OCPP stations included in eCarUp. (If it does, please call us before deleting.)

- Click your username (top right) and then Settings (Einstellungen)

- Click Delete user (Benutzer löschen)

</details>
