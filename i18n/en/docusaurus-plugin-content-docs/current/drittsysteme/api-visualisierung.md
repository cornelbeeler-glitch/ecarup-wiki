## Arcade Solution AG

Winkelriedstrasse 37

6003 Luzern

Contact: [sales@arcade.ch](mailto:sales@arcade.ch)

Website: [https://www.arcade.ch](https://www.arcade.ch)

![API Visualization – Figure 1](/img/drittsysteme-api-visualisierung/01.png)

Arcade Solution AG is a Swiss company specializing in ICT solutions. The eCarUp integration lets you do the following:

- Create your own dashboards

- Optional integration of parking sensors, whose data can be combined and analyzed together with the data from eCarUp

- General analyses of the charge points and of revenue

- The flexible interface makes many more use cases possible in the future


Here you can find a practical example that has already been implemented by a joint project partner: 

- [City of Lucerne](https://www.ewl-luzern.ch/no_cache/privatkunden/dienstleistungen/mobilitaet/pilotprojekt-gruene-zonen/)

Example: The analysis of the charging stations of smart-me AG. The Pico charging stations are operated in a mix of public and limited use cases.

At the publicly available charging stations, money is collected, and at the limited stations, employees can charge free of charge.
