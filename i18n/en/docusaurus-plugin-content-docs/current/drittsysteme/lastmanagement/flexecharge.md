FLEXeCHARGE gives you brand-independent load management based on API and OCPP. 

eCarUp gives FLEXeCHARGE priority for control and handles billing for the installations efficiently.

![FLEXeCHARGE – Figure 1](/img/drittsysteme-lastmanagement-flexecharge/01.png)

Focus: Large residential developments and installations with high load management requirements

### Complete

The solution focuses on comprehensive load management for large infrastructures that need local control and integration of building consumption, including PV and battery systems.

The solution can also read smart-me meters directly and use them for control.

### Independent

Stay flexible when expanding your e-mobility infrastructure. FLEXeCHARGE can be used independently of brand; the prerequisite is an API interface or OCPP.

### Reliable

Thanks to local management with the FLEXeCHARGE hardware, the system is always in operation and unaffected by interruptions in the internet connection.

### Transparent

With the FLEXeCHARGE platform, all charging stations can be managed centrally and remotely. React quickly and save service costs.

### Future-proof

FLEXeCHARGE focuses exclusively on smart charging with existing, commercially available hardware. 
The development of Plug & Charge, V2H and V2G functions is the top priority.
