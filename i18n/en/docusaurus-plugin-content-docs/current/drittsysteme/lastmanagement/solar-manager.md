Focus: load management in single-family homes up to larger apartment buildings

### Complete

Coordinate the control of solar inverters, battery systems, water heaters and e-mobility. The central focus is to maximise self-consumption. Optimised charging curves based on self-generated energy, or peak shaving by timing the consumers.

### Brand-independent

Solar Manager integrates products from the current market as required, significantly improving interoperability. For charging stations, a wide range of integrated charging stations is available.

Compatibility list: [https://www.solarmanager.ch/produkt/unterstuetzte-geraete/](https://www.solarmanager.ch/produkt/unterstuetzte-geraete/)

The smart-me meter products and the smart-me Pico charging station are of course also included.

### Reliable

Load management by Solar Manager runs locally on the gateway and can be monitored via the cloud. The clear display of the individual phase loads helps you make improvements to the infrastructure.

### Future-oriented

With its sole focus on efficient and intelligent control, Solar Manager is a future-oriented partner for controlling building infrastructure.
