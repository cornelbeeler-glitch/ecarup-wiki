CLEMAP Load Management handles real-time load management of AC and DC charging stations from various manufacturers and combines ideally with the eCarUp billing portal.

Important: load management does not take place via OCPP

It is a very simple and reliable load management solution that integrates easily and seamlessly into the charging infrastructure for electric cars.

![CLEMAP – Figure 1](/img/drittsysteme-lastmanagement-clemap/01.png)

Focus: load management in apartment buildings and industry

### Manufacturer-independent

The system is compatible with AC and DC wallboxes and charging stations from various manufacturers.

List of compatible products: [https://www.clemap.com/dynamisches-lastmanagement#kompatibilitaet](https://www.clemap.com/dynamisches-lastmanagement#kompatibilitaet)

### Flexible

Any number of charging stations can be integrated, including at a later stage.

Integration of controllable and non-controllable charging stations (ON/OFF charging stations). This includes the smart-me Pico charging station with stepless control.

### Stable and with popular features

- Operation with or without a connection to the cloud

- Remote setting of available power, prioritization of charging stations and charging with solar energy

- Support for multiple electrical circuits


### Cost-efficient

One-time, low investment costs for metering and operating the system.

### Fast commissioning

Installation and activation within a few hours.

### Remote monitoring and control

Convenient configuration from the «office» or on the go via desktop access and an online portal with multiple users and hierarchical access levels.

More information: [https://www.clemap.com/dynamisches-lastmanagement](https://www.clemap.com/dynamisches-lastmanagement)
