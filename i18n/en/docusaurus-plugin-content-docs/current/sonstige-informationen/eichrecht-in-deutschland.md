## Digitally signed measured values

The transmission of digitally signed measured values is a basic requirement that a charging facility must meet to comply with calibration law (Eichrecht) in Germany. If the charging facility supports this functionality, you can verify the transactions at the station at any time using the process described below. This also allows the station to be operated semi-publicly or publicly via eCarUp.

### Verifying a signature with eCarUp

1.  Log in to the driver account on [www.ecarup.com](http://www.ecarup.com) for which you want to verify a charging session. In the navigation on the left, click the menu item Driver (Fahrer) and then History.

2.  For the desired charging session, click the fingerprint icon (signature) on the far right.

3.  A window opens showing the signed measured values and the public key of the charging station. Alternatively, you can also find the public key on the charging station (usually on the front of the meter).

4.  Download the transparency software from the manufacturer of the charging station (see below) and enter the signed measured values and the public key. 

5.  The charging session is validated digitally.

### Verifying digitally signed measured values of the Pico charging station

You can retrieve the signed measurement data of the Pico charging station directly from the smart-me platform and verify it with the S.A.F.E transparency software.

1.  Log in to [www.smart-me.com](http://www.smart-me.com) 

2.  Select the Pico

3.  At the top right, click signed meter readings and transactions (signierte Zählerstände und Transaktionen)


[More information](https://wiki.smart-me.com/informationssicherheit/zaehlertransaktionen)
