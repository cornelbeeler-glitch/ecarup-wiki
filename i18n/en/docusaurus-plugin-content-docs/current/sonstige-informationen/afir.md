Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation

To make the metering point public and receive the EVSE ID, you need the [eCarUp Premium](https://web.ecarup.com/preise-ladestationen-backend/) feature. 

You can purchase the licences online. You can find more information here: [LINK](/stationsbetreiber/lizenzen)

## General

The AFIR (Alternative Fuels Infrastructure Regulation) is an EU regulation intended to standardise the expansion of charging stations and hydrogen refuelling stations across Europe. It has been in force since April 2024 and above all brings concrete requirements for operators of charging infrastructure (CPOs) and their backend systems. 

## Germany

### General

The Bundesnetzagentur is the official register of charging stations for Germany. 

The AFIR requires member states to operate a "National Access Point" (NAP). In Germany, this is the Mobilithek. 

The register allows electric car drivers to find the nearest public charging stations on a map and in a list. It provides detailed information on location, connector types, charging power, opening hours and roaming options.

As the backend operator of publicly accessible charging stations, eCarUp (we as the platform developer) is legally obliged to transmit the data of your charging station to the Mobilithek. This ensures that all public and roaming-enabled charging stations in Germany are registered and can be found by electric car drivers.  

We transmit the data both statically and dynamically. 

Dynamic example: 

- Current availability (whether a charge point is free, occupied or reserved.)


Static example:

- Charging power

- Connector types

- EVSE ID

- Current type (AC or DC)

- etc.


### What do I need to do for the charging station to be transmitted to the Mobilithek?

- Access must be public

- The location must be configured. 

- The charge point must have a price

- You can find the guide here: [Station management](/funktionen/stations-verwaltung) 


### How can I check whether the data is being transmitted to the Mobilithek?

- If the charging station is on our map [https://www.ecarup.com/map/ecarupmap.html](https://www.ecarup.com/map/ecarupmap.html) 


- It can take up to 2 working days until the data is visible on the [Bundesnetzagentur](https://www.bundesnetzagentur.de/DE/Fachthemen/ElektrizitaetundGas/E-Mobilitaet/Ladesaeulenkarte/Karte/start.html) map
