## Why do eCarUp station owners have to verify their accounts?

eCarUp AG never holds the funds transferred via our platform. They belong either to the credit card holder (driver) or directly to the station owner. This means eCarUp is not a financial intermediary and does not manage third-party assets. For such transactions (including at the scale processed via eCarUp), legislators in Switzerland and Europe require that the beneficial ownership of the funds be established. The credit transferred to the station owners' accounts therefore legally belongs to the verified person (legal or natural) and this person is responsible for correct handling and booking as well as for protection against misuse.

You can find more information here [on billing with a credit card.](/stationsbetreiber/abrechnen-auszahlen)

All financial transactions are processed via our payment service provider Stripe.  It is a certified and established online payment service with a global presence. So when you pay with a credit card at eCarUp, the standard processes for validation, data transmission security and data protection take place in the background. In addition, the transactions comply with anti-money laundering and banking laws throughout Europe. Learn more about security at Stripe here: [Data security and data protection at Stripe](https://stripe.com/docs/security/stripe) (in English).

NOTES: 

- We only collect the minimum information required by law. This information is, of course, treated in the strictest confidence and is not used in any other way or passed on to third parties.

- The legal provisions on money laundering and transparency may change and may therefore also require an adjustment on the eCarUp platform.


### Extension of the Know-Your-Customer principle (Fifth EU Anti-Money Laundering Directive)

As part of the fight against terrorist financing, the EU is tightening transparency in financial transactions in 2020 with the implementation of the Fifth Anti-Money Laundering Directive. For eCarUp station owners, this means that the identity of the account owner (private individual or legal entity) must be established at all times and verified by entering identification information. You can find more information on the directive on this [page](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/de/STATEMENT_18_3429).
