## Please proceed as follows:

1.   Log in to the eCarUp web portal and go to Account (Account).




2.  Click Legal information (Rechtliche Angaben) and add your company's VAT number. 




3.  Then go to Balance history (Saldoverlauf) and look for your most recent payout. 




4.  Click "Resend" ("Erneut senden").
