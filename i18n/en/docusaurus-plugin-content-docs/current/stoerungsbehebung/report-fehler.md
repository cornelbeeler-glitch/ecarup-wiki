This page explains what to do if there are differences between the payout and the report.

## Differences in the payout

⚠️ Important: You need an eCarUp Premium licence to create a report.

If there is a difference between the report and the payout, you need to find out the reason for it.
To do this, create a "CSV Extended" ("CSV-Erweitert") report. 

Keep the following in mind:

- The date of your last payout

- The time of your current payout


![Report errors – Figure 1](/img/stoerungsbehebung-report-fehler/01.png)

For an explanation of the terms used in the report, see this page.

In short, the important part is the payment status: 

- Paid = paid – the driver has paid for the charging session and everything is correct.

- notCharged = amount too small – no billing has taken place.

- Failed = the money cannot be collected from the customer.




Differences usually occur when some drivers or charging sessions show "notCharged" or "Failed".

Add up the amounts and compare the total with the billing. The amount should then match.
