### Note on Apple iPhone (iOS):

Android recognises almost every chip type. Apple, however, heavily restricts the NFC interface: older or proprietary chips (e.g. Mifare Classic or Legic), which are often used on charging cards, are blocked by iOS for security reasons. eCarUp has no influence on this.

If your iPhone does not recognise the card:

- Enter it manually: Simply type the card number printed on the card (UID, if available) into the eCarUp app by hand (without spaces).

- Register it via Android: Log in once to your eCarUp account on an Android device and scan the card there. It is then immediately added to your account.
